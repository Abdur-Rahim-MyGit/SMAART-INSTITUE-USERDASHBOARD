# Navbar Layout Update

**Date**: December 5, 2025 - 11:55 AM IST  
**Objective**: Adjust navbar layout to provide more space and move content "to the left".  
**Status**: ✅ COMPLETED

---

## 🔧 The Change

### **Wider Container**
- **Old**: `max-width: 1200px`
- **New**: `max-width: 1400px`
- **Effect**: By increasing the maximum width, the **Logo** pushes further to the **Left** and the **Menu** pushes further to the **Right**. This utilizes more of the screen real estate on modern monitors, making the navbar feel less "centered" and constrained.

## 🚀 How to Verify
1.  **Maximize your browser**.
2.  Notice that the **Logo** is now closer to the left edge of the screen than before.
3.  The menu items have more breathing room.
