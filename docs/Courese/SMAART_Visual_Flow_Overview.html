Four Entry Pathways
Every student is automatically assigned a pathway at onboarding based on their programme type, year, and work experience. T1 is always the first step regardless.
STANDARD
UG students joining Year 1, Sem 1. The default pathway.
3–4 years · Full programme · Track A SJTs
ACCELERATED
PG/MBA students with 12+ months work experience.
2 years compressed · Track B SJTs (harder professional scenarios)
FOUNDATION
PG/MBA students with under 12 months experience (fresh MBA).
2 years · Mixed SJT difficulty · Full T4 professional-level
CATCH_UP
UG late joiners — Year 2, 3, or 4 entry.
Remaining semesters · Priority Pathway algorithm · Lighter T1 (14 SJTs)
