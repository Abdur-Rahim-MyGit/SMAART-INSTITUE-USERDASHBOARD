How AI is changing this role
What is being automated, and what the analyst now owns instead
  • memory
    Data cleaning and preparation is largely automated

    AI tools profile, deduplicate and reshape raw data in minutes. The analyst reviews and approves rather than scripts it by hand.

  • analytics
    Dashboards and standard reports build themselves

    Natural-language queries generate charts and summaries. Value shifts to choosing the right question and checking the answer.

  • neurology
    Insight and interpretation stay human

    Explaining why a number moved, what the business should do, and what the data cannot tell you is where analysts now spend most of their time.

  • verified
    New responsibility: checking the machine

    Validating AI output, spotting hallucinated figures and documenting data lineage is becoming a core part of the job description.

  • forum
    More time with stakeholders, less with spreadsheets

    Roles are moving closer to product, finance and marketing teams, with the analyst acting as a translator between data and decisions.
