# 🧠 SMAART Toolkit: Complete Feature & AI Guide

This document provides a clear breakdown of every tool in the **SMAART Toolkit** and the specific AI models/technologies powering them.

---

## 🚀 AI-Powered Tools
These tools leverage state-of-the-art Large Language Models (LLMs) to provide personalized, intelligent career support.

### 1. 🤖 AI Career Chat
*   **What it does**: Direct, real-time conversation with an intelligent career strategist. It helps you brainstorm career moves, prepare for interviews, and understand niche industry trends.
*   **AI Model**: **NVIDIA Nemotron-3-Nano (30B)** 
*   **Key Features**:
    *   24/7 strategic guidance.
    *   Remembers your context for personalized advice.
    *   Suggests "Next Steps" to keep you moving forward.

### 2. 📊 AI Profile Analysis
*   **What it does**: Analyzes your current skills, education, and goals to identify exactly where you stand in the job market.
*   **AI Model**: **NVIDIA Nemotron-3-Nano (30B)**
*   **Key Features**:
    *   **Skill Gap Analysis**: Tells you what skills you are missing for your dream job.
    *   **Trajectory Visualization**: Shows you potential career paths based on your *actual* data.
    *   **Learning Plan**: Generates a custom roadmap to bridge your gaps.

### 3. 📄 SMAART AI Resume Builder
*   **What it does**: Automatically crafts professional, ATS-optimized resumes that highlight your strengths most effectively to recruiters.
*   **AI Model**: **NVIDIA Nemotron-3-Nano (30B)**
*   **Key Features**:
    *   **Smart Summaries**: AI creates high-impact professional summaries.
    *   **Role-Specific Content**: Optimizes your bullet points for the specific job you want.
    *   **Action-Oriented Language**: Uses strong professional verbs (Action + Task + Result).

## 📚 Resource & Wellness Tools
These tools use real-time global databases and management systems to provide essential resources and support.

### 4. 🧘 Mind Care Sessions
*   **What it does**: Connects you with certified coaches for personalized wellness and growth sessions.
*   **Technology**: **Coach Routing & Booking Engine**.
*   **Categories**:
    *   **Academic**: Study strategies and help with complex assignments.
    *   **Career**: Deep professional growth and job transition support.
    *   **Mental Health**: Stress management, anxiety support, and self-care.
    *   **Personal**: Life skills, goal setting, and confidence building.

### 5. 📖 Knowledge Library
*   **What it does**: A massive search engine for books and professional learning materials.
*   **Technology**: **Google Books API (Live Integration)**.
*   **Key Features**:
    *   Millions of books searchable by Title, Author, or ISBN.
    *   Real-time book covers and "Read Preview" links.
    *   Curated offline "Standard Library" for career development.

### 6. 📚 General Dictionary & Thesaurus
*   **What it does**: A professional terminology reference tool with voice support.
*   **Technology**: **Dual-API System** (DictionaryAPI.dev + Datamuse).
*   **Key Features**:
    *   **Definitions & Examples**: Full meanings and usage in real-world sentences.
    *   **Synonym Engine**: Find relevant alternatives to expand your professional vocabulary.
    *   **Native Audio**: Listen to the correct pronunciation of complex terms.
