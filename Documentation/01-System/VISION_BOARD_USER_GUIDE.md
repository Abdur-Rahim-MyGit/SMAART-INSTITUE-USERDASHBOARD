## 1. Getting Started

### What is a Vision Board?

A vision board is a collage of images and words that represent your goals and dreams. Looking at your vision board regularly helps you stay focused and motivated to achieve your aspirations.

### Before You Begin

Make sure you have:

- ✓ Created an account on the SMAART Institute platform
- ✓ Completed the assessment flow (if required)
- ✓ Logged into your account

## 2. Creating Your First Vision Board

1. Log in to your account
2. From the dashboard, click on **"Vision Boards"** in the sidebar menu

Adjust the canvas settings to your liking:

- **Aspect Ratio**: Choose 1:1 (square), 4:5, 16:9, or others
- **Background Color**: Pick a color that matches your theme
- **Border Radius**: Rounded or sharp corners
- **Gap**: Space between image slots

### Step 5: Add Images

1. Click on an image slot in your template
2. Upload an image from your device OR select from provided options
3. The image will automatically fit into the slot

**Supported formats**: JPG, PNG, GIF (max 5MB per image)


### Step 6: Add Text (Optional)

1. Click the **"Add Text"** button
2. Type your motivational word or phrase
3. Customize:
   - Font style and size
   - Color
   - Position on the board

### Step 7: Save Your Vision Board

1. Click **"Save"** or **"Create"** button
2. Give your vision board a title (e.g., "My 2024 Goals")
3. Add an optional description
4. Confirm save

### Available Actions

| Action | What It Does |
|--------|--------------|
| **View** | Open and preview your vision board |
| **Edit** | Modify images, text, or settings |
| **Duplicate** | Create a copy of the board |
| **Set as Active** | Display on your dashboard |
| **Delete** | Remove the board permanently |
| **Download** | Save as an image file |

### Editing a Board

1. Click **"Edit"** on any board
2. Make your changes:
   - Replace images
   - Change text
   - Adjust layout
3. Click **"Save Changes"**

### Deleting a Board

⚠️ **Warning**: This action cannot be undone!

1. Click **"Delete"** on the board
2. Confirm the deletion in the popup
3. The board will be permanently removed

## 4. Using the Dashboard Widget

### What is the Dashboard Widget?

The Vision Board widget appears on your main dashboard. It displays your "active" vision board - the one you want to see every day!

### Setting an Active Vision

1. Go to your Vision Boards gallery
2. Find the board you want to display
3. Click **"Set as Active"**

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ┌──────────┐                                  │
│  │ ┌──────┐ │  [Set as Active] 👈 Click!     │
│  │ │      │ │                                  │
│  │ │ IMG  │ │                                  │
│  │ │      │ │                                  │
│  │ └──────┘ │                                  │
│  └──────────┘                                  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Viewing on Dashboard

Once set as active, your vision board will appear:

- On the main dashboard (if enabled)
- In the Vision Board splash screen when you log in

### Changing Your Active Vision

You can change your active vision at any time:

1. Go to any of your boards
2. Click **"Set as Active"**
3. The new board will replace the old one

### Clearing Active Vision

To remove the vision board from your dashboard:

1. Go to your Vision Boards gallery
2. Click **"Clear Active"** (or similar option)

## 5. Tips for Success

### Creating an Effective Vision Board

1. **Be Specific**
   - ❌ "I want to be happy"
   - ✓ "I will run a marathon in 2024"

2. **Use Positive Images**
   - Choose pictures that make you feel good
   - Avoid negative or stressful images

3. **Include Action Words**
   - Add text like "ACHIEVE," "SUCCESS," "HEALTHY"
   - Use present tense: "I AM," "I HAVE"

4. **Arrange Meaningfully**
   - Group related goals together
   - Place most important goals in the center

5. **Review Regularly**
   - Look at your board daily
   - It keeps your goals fresh in your mind

## 6. Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### Issue: Can't See My Vision Boards

**Solution:**

- Make sure you're logged in
- Check that you've created at least one board
- Try refreshing the page

#### Issue: Images Won't Upload

**Possible Causes:**

- File too large (max 5MB)
- Unsupported format (use JPG, PNG, or GIF)

**Solution:**

- Compress your image before uploading
- Convert to a supported format
- Try a smaller image

#### Issue: "Set as Active" Not Working

**Solution:**

- Wait a moment and try again
- Refresh the page
- Make sure the board is saved

#### Issue: Can't Create More Boards

**Cause:** You're limited to 3 Vision Boards (Pro version)

**Solution:**

- Delete an existing board to make room
- Or duplicate a board to modify it

#### Issue: Dashboard Widget Not Showing

**Solution:**

- Make sure a board is set as "Active"
- Check your internet connection
- Clear browser cache and refresh

### Getting Help

If you continue to experience issues:

1. Check the FAQ section
2. Contact support through the Help option
3. Ask your administrator
